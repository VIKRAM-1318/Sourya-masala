Our story

Built around the everyday work of making good food.

Sourya Masala Pvt Ltd is based in Kuppam, Andhra Pradesh. Founder Surya Prakash Raman started the company with a simple aim: make dependable Indian spice blends available to kitchens and businesses that need them in practical quantities.

We pair familiar regional flavours with a straightforward approach to supply: clear conversations, considered packing and attention to consistency.
